# Professional Narrative

## The short version

I turn complex growth environments into scalable operating systems.

Across 15+ years, I have led performance media, CRO, measurement, marketing operations, agency governance, AI-enabled workflows, and executive decision support. I am typically most useful when growth is important, the environment is moving quickly, and the operating model underneath performance has become too fragmented to scale cleanly.

My job is to reconnect the system: investment, demand, conversion, measurement, agencies, teams, technology, and leadership decisions.

## The fuller version

I am a growth systems leader with deep performance-media operating expertise. I build the infrastructure behind scalable demand and the decision systems that tell leadership where to invest, what to fix, and what to stop.

That means operating comfortably across strategy and execution. I can move from campaign architecture, funnel quality, and signal QA to capital allocation, agency economics, organizational design, CRO, measurement, and executive decision support without losing the business thread.

When I enter a complex growth environment, I first make the system legible. I map how demand actually moves, identify where performance is leaking, determine which signals are trustworthy, separate channel problems from funnel and operating-model problems, and clarify the decisions leadership actually needs to make.

Then I build the layer that makes improvement repeatable: allocation logic, measurement standards, decision rights, agency accountability, QA, reporting, operating cadence, playbooks, and workflows that teams can run without depending on individual heroics.

## What I am best hired to own

- Enterprise performance and growth leadership across brands, channels, agencies, and internal teams
- Media investment strategy and capital allocation across $15M+ recent portfolio scope and $100M+ historical global programs
- Full-funnel growth systems connecting performance media, CRO, measurement, CRM, enrollment, and revenue operations
- Growth transformation where execution has outgrown the operating model
- Agency governance, SOWs, scope, accountability, economics, escalation, and in-housing decisions
- Marketing operations and AI-enabled infrastructure that increase operating leverage
- Executive decision support that turns performance noise into clear tradeoffs, risks, and recommended actions
- Team and operating-model design that creates durable capability rather than temporary performance spikes

## The throughline

At global agencies, I led planning, measurement, enablement, and operating frameworks across complex enterprise accounts and $100M+ programs. At Stand Together, I rebuilt paid media operations for a large advocacy and philanthropic network. At NP Digital, I restructured its largest paid media division during rapid growth and operational reset. At Pansophic Learning, I led the growth operating system connecting $15M+ in media, CRO, measurement, agency governance, and investment decisions across 180+ institutions.

Different environments. Same leadership problem: growth becomes harder to manage as complexity increases.

I make that complexity legible, turn it into decisions, and build the operating system that allows the organization to scale with more control.
