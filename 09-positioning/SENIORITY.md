# Seniority Lens

Employer-facing positioning should show the level of problem Jared can own.

Where supported, foreground:

- capital and portfolio allocation decisions;
- enterprise and global scale;
- cross-functional operating-model design;
- agency and partner accountability;
- team and organizational leadership;
- full-funnel and downstream business judgment;
- executive decision support;
- repeatable systems that create operating leverage.

Do not collapse these signals into channel execution simply because tactical evidence is easier to quantify.
