# Truth-to-Value Translation

A supported fact becomes strong positioning when the evaluator understands why it matters.

Examples:

- **$15M+ media across 180+ institutions** → portfolio-scale investment and operating complexity.
- **$500K reallocation** → willingness and ability to make material capital-allocation tradeoffs.
- **~500% approved-application growth** → acquisition-system diagnosis and productive-output improvement.
- **$100M+ global programs / 30+ person teams** → leadership across scale, disciplines, regions, and organizational complexity.
- **150+ landing pages / five CRO workstreams** → CRO as operating infrastructure rather than isolated testing.
- **AI operating systems** → practical leverage across monitoring, synthesis, QA, context, and decision support.

Translation may explain significance. It may not invent a downstream financial result or causal relationship that the evidence does not support.
