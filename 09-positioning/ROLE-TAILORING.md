# Role Tailoring Rule

A generic public artifact can establish the platform, but final application positioning must be rescored against the actual role.

Tailoring should change:

- evidence selection;
- sequencing;
- competency emphasis;
- employer-value translation;
- vocabulary and level of detail.

Tailoring must not change:

- facts;
- chronology;
- ownership;
- metric level;
- causal authority;
- qualifications.

A generic Tier 1 artifact is not automatically Tier 1 for every job. The final tailored artifact must still clear 80/100 after role requirements are applied.
