# Employer-Facing Positioning Contract

## Authority split

The public portfolio has two complementary layers:

1. **Evidence authority** controls factual admissibility, metric level, ownership, causality, chronology, and public safety.
2. **Positioning authority** selects and communicates the strongest relevant supported truth for the employer or evaluator.

The first prevents exaggeration. The second prevents unnecessary self-disqualification.

Neither may replace the other.

## Optimization target

Employer-facing material should maximize the probability that a qualified evaluator understands Jared's relevant value, seniority, scope, judgment, and differentiated capability.

This optimization is always subject to the evidence authority. No positioning objective permits invented facts, promoted funnel metrics, unsupported causality, inflated ownership, or altered chronology.

## Required sequence

1. Resolve the evaluator's actual question or role requirement.
2. Retrieve admitted evidence.
3. Establish the hard factual boundary internally.
4. Select the strongest evidence that closes the requirement.
5. Translate the evidence into employer value.
6. Write in persuasive senior candidate voice.
7. Check specificity and proof.
8. Run adversarial factual QA.
9. Remove unnecessary defensive language.
10. Release only at Tier 1 quality.

## Defensiveness rule

Do not foreground what Jared did not own merely because the evidence system knows that boundary.

Surface a limitation when:

- the evaluator explicitly asks about it;
- omitting it would create a materially misleading inference;
- the distinction is necessary to interpret the metric correctly; or
- legal, compensation, authorization, EEO, or other hard-policy handling requires it.

Otherwise, preserve the boundary in governance metadata and lead with the supported achievement.

## Translation examples

Instead of centering: "Approved applications are not revenue or profit."

Center: "Approved applications grew approximately 500% after the rebuild, materially increasing the productive output of the acquisition system."

The first statement remains an evidence boundary. The second is the employer-facing thesis.

Instead of centering: "$15M+ is scale, not formal enterprise P&L ownership."

Center: "Led growth work connecting $15M+ in media across 180+ institutions with CRO, measurement, agency governance, and portfolio investment decisions."

The ownership boundary remains enforced underneath the narrative.

## Final test

Before release ask two separate questions:

**Truth test:** Is every material claim supportable at the level stated?

**Hireability test:** Given everything supportably true, is this the strongest relevant case we can make for trusting Jared with the employer's problem?

Both must pass.
