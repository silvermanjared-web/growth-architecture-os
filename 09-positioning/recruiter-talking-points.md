# Recruiter Talking Points

## One-line frame

I turn complex growth environments into scalable operating systems.

## Executive positioning

I am a growth systems leader with deep performance-media expertise. I lead across investment strategy, acquisition, CRO, measurement, agency governance, marketing operations, AI-enabled workflows, and executive decision support.

My strongest work happens when a company has real demand and meaningful investment but the growth system has become fragmented. I make performance legible, identify the highest-leverage decisions, align the teams and partners around them, and build the operating infrastructure required to scale.

## Proof of scale

- $15M+ recent media scope across 180+ institutions
- $500K portfolio media reallocation across priority markets
- $100M+ historical global programs
- 30+ person teams across media, analytics, and strategy
- ~500% growth in approved applications after a phased WEX acquisition rebuild
- 86% YoY growth in Stand Together web-qualified engagements while outperforming benchmarks by 3x

## What I am best hired to solve

- Growth organizations that need a senior operator across strategy and execution
- Multi-brand or multi-market portfolios where investment decisions have become difficult to govern
- Performance programs that need stronger downstream economics, measurement, and conversion discipline
- Agency ecosystems that need clearer accountability, scope, standards, and decision rights
- CRO and funnel programs that need to become operating capabilities rather than isolated tests
- Leadership teams that need clearer visibility into performance, risk, tradeoffs, and where capital should move
- Marketing organizations that want practical AI operating leverage rather than disconnected experimentation

## What makes me different

I can move from campaign architecture and signal QA to capital allocation, agency economics, operating-model design, organizational leadership, and executive decision support without losing the business thread.

That combination matters because growth problems rarely stay inside one channel. I am strongest when the role requires someone to see the whole system, decide what matters, and create enough structure for the organization to execute against it.

## Questions to ask recruiters

- What business problem is this role being hired to solve?
- What would leadership need this person to change in the first 6 to 12 months?
- How much authority does the role have over investment, measurement, partners, team design, and operating process?
- Is the organization looking for a channel leader, a growth operator, or someone to redesign how the growth system works?
