# Role Targeting

This portfolio is built to demonstrate senior growth leadership: the ability to connect investment, acquisition, conversion, measurement, partners, teams, technology, and executive decisions into one scalable operating system.

## Primary role families

- VP Growth
- VP Performance Marketing
- Head of Growth
- Head of Performance Marketing
- VP / Senior Director Growth Strategy
- Growth Transformation Leader
- Growth Operations / Marketing Operations Leader
- AI-enabled marketing operations leader

## Environments where I create the most value

- PE-backed or high-growth companies where growth complexity is increasing faster than the operating model
- Multi-brand, multi-market, or portfolio businesses requiring disciplined capital allocation
- Organizations with meaningful paid-media investment and weak connection between acquisition and downstream business outcomes
- Companies managing multiple agencies or redesigning the agency / in-house model
- Businesses with fragmented measurement, CRO, CRM, reporting, or funnel ownership
- Leadership teams that need clearer performance visibility and faster decision-making
- Marketing organizations looking to turn AI from experimentation into practical operating leverage

## Value proposition

I make complex growth systems easier to run and easier to scale.

I connect capital allocation, performance media, CRO, measurement, agencies, marketing operations, and executive decision support so leadership can see what is happening, understand what matters, and move resources with more confidence.

The result is not simply cleaner marketing operations. It is a stronger growth organization: clearer ownership, better signal quality, more disciplined investment decisions, stronger partner accountability, faster escalation, and repeatable execution.

## Positioning frame

The narrow frame is: **I run paid media.**

The senior frame is: **I lead the growth operating system that determines how investment, acquisition, conversion, measurement, agencies, teams, and leadership decisions work together.**

Performance media is a core area of depth. The value proposition is broader: using that depth to lead the system around growth.

## Role-selection principle

Prioritize roles where the mandate includes meaningful ownership over growth strategy, investment, measurement, partners, operating model, or cross-functional execution. The strongest fit is an organization that needs both strategic judgment and an operator who can translate that judgment into a working system.
