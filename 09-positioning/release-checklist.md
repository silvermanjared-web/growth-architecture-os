# Positioning Release Checklist

Use this checklist for new or materially revised employer-facing public artifacts.

- [ ] The evaluator or role requirement is clear.
- [ ] Material claims resolve to admitted evidence.
- [ ] The artifact leads with capability, result, scale, judgment, or employer value.
- [ ] The strongest relevant supported evidence is selected.
- [ ] Metrics remain at their supported funnel level.
- [ ] Ownership and causality are not inflated.
- [ ] Evidence is translated into what it demonstrates for the employer.
- [ ] Seniority is visible through scope, decisions, systems, teams, capital, or organizational leverage where supported.
- [ ] Internal governance language has not leaked into candidate voice.
- [ ] No objection is answered unless the evaluator raised it or omission would mislead.
- [ ] The writing sounds like Jared rather than a compliance memo.
- [ ] The artifact is specific enough to be memorable.
- [ ] The artifact is concise enough for the intended surface.
- [ ] Adversarial factual QA passes.
- [ ] Hireability score is at least 80/100 (Tier 1).

**Release condition:** truth test PASS + hireability test Tier 1.
