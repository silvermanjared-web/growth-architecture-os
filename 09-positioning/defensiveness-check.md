# Defensiveness QA Gate

Before releasing employer-facing material, check for a specific failure mode: factual caution becoming the candidate's thesis.

Rewrite when the artifact:

- leads with what Jared did not own;
- volunteers a limitation the evaluator did not ask about;
- uses evidence-governance language as candidate voice;
- spends more words defending a metric than explaining why it matters;
- reduces senior scope because narrower wording feels safer; or
- describes evidence without translating it into employer value.

Do not rewrite away a boundary that is necessary to prevent a materially misleading inference.

The target is confident, specific, senior, and supportable.
