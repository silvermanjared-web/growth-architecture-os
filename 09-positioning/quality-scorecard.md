## Interpretation

The material now clears the release threshold because it does four things simultaneously:

1. preserves the factual authority and evidence boundaries;
2. leads with the senior problems Jared can own;
3. translates proof into employer value rather than merely displaying metrics; and
4. keeps internal governance mechanics subordinate to the public narrative.

The remaining headroom is intentional. A generic public artifact cannot receive full role-alignment credit against every future job. Final application materials should be rescored after the actual role requirements are applied.
