# Hireability Positioning Audit

## Release objective

Optimize employer-facing public positioning for the strongest credible case for hiring Jared Silverman while preserving the existing governed evidence system as the factual authority.

## Scoring contract

Tier 1 requires 80/100 or better across the weighted model defined in `hireability-quality-standard.md`.

The audit is intentionally different from factual admission. The claim registry answers **may we say this?** The hireability audit answers **given what we may truthfully say, are we making the strongest relevant case?**

## Governing conclusion

The public portfolio should optimize for hireability, not indisputability.

That does **not** mean weakening factual standards. The evidence layer remains authoritative and conservative about unsupported causality, ownership, revenue, profit, titles, and funnel promotion.

The change is in presentation: employer-facing artifacts select the strongest relevant supported truth, translate it into business value, and communicate it with appropriate seniority. Evidence controls remain underneath that narrative and surface only when material.

## Recurring defect removed

The previous public architecture sometimes exposed internal control language directly to evaluators, including formulations centered on bounded evidence, prohibited inference, causal restraint, or what a metric did not prove.

Those distinctions remain available in the claim system. They no longer need to dominate the first impression.

## Release principle

**Never answer the objection the employer has not raised.**

Accuracy is the constraint. Hireability is the objective.
