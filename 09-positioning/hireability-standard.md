# Hireability Positioning Standard

Public career-facing material in this portfolio is optimized for one objective: **make the strongest credible case for hiring Jared for the relevant senior growth role.**

Truth is non-negotiable. It defines the admissible evidence set and factual boundary. Within that boundary, positioning should maximize relevance, seniority, employer value, specificity, and persuasive force.

## 100-point quality model

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Truth & evidence | 20 |
| Role-requirement alignment | 15 |
| Intended communication | 10 |
| Jared voice | 10 |
| Truth-to-value translation | 15 |
| Persuasion / convincingness | 15 |
| Specificity & proof | 10 |
| Clarity & economy | 5 |

**Tier 1 = 80–100.** Career-facing positioning below 80 requires revision before release.

## Release sequence

**Evidence → hard factual boundary → role context → strongest relevant truth → employer value → persuasive candidate voice → adversarial QA → release.**

## Required behaviors

- Lead with capability, scale, result, or employer value rather than a caveat.
- Translate evidence into what it demonstrates the candidate can do in the target organization.
- Preserve seniority. Do not collapse enterprise operating leadership into channel execution simply because channel evidence is easier to quantify.
- Prefer concrete proof over generic adjectives.
- Keep evidence-governance controls in metadata, registries, or review layers unless the distinction is material to the employer's question.
- Never answer an objection the employer has not raised.
- Never strengthen a metric, funnel stage, ownership claim, causal relationship, title, or qualification beyond the evidence.
- When a requested claim is unsupported, answer the underlying competency with the strongest adjacent supported truth rather than leading with what cannot be claimed.

## Defensiveness check

Before release, ask:

1. Does the opening tell the reader why this person is valuable?
2. Is any limitation receiving more prominence than the supported achievement?
3. Does governance language sound like candidate voice?
4. Are we prosecuting an objection the employer did not raise?
5. Could the same facts be sequenced to communicate greater seniority or business consequence without changing their truth value?

If yes to 2, 3, or 4, rewrite.

## Governance relationship

The claim registry remains authoritative for factual admissibility. This standard governs **selection, sequencing, translation, and presentation** of admitted evidence.

The two layers solve different problems:

- **Evidence governance:** What can be said truthfully?
- **Positioning governance:** Given what can be said truthfully, what makes the strongest credible case for hiring Jared?

Both are required.
