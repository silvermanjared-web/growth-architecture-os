# Role Targeting and Employer Value

## Core positioning

Jared is best positioned for senior growth leadership roles where the mandate extends beyond channel execution into investment, conversion, measurement, partners, operating design, and executive decision-making.

The strongest frame is:

> I build and lead the growth operating system that connects capital, acquisition, conversion, measurement, agencies, teams, and leadership decisions.

## Priority role families

### VP / Head of Growth

**Employer need:** A leader who can own a fragmented growth environment as one system rather than optimize isolated channels.

**Relevant proof:** $15M+ recent media scope across 180+ institutions, $500K portfolio reallocation, CRO and measurement operating models, agency governance, and downstream funnel decision-making.

**Value translation:** Jared can enter a complex environment, determine where growth is actually breaking, make investment and operating-model decisions, and establish a repeatable cadence for scaling.

### VP / Head of Performance Marketing

**Employer need:** Senior acquisition leadership with enough operating depth to connect media decisions to conversion quality and business economics.

**Relevant proof:** ~500% growth in approved applications after the WEX rebuild, $100M+ historical global programs, multi-channel operating models, agency leadership, measurement, QA, and allocation systems.

**Value translation:** Jared brings both performance depth and the executive operating layer required to manage large-scale acquisition as capital rather than a collection of platform tactics.

### Growth Strategy / Transformation Leadership

**Employer need:** A leader who can stabilize an underperforming or overcomplicated growth organization and build the infrastructure for the next stage.

**Relevant proof:** enterprise operating-model work, NP Digital organizational reset, Stand Together media rebuild, portfolio governance, first-90-day framework, and reusable playbooks.

**Value translation:** Jared is strongest where the company needs diagnosis, prioritization, cross-functional alignment, and a durable operating model rather than another layer of activity.

### AI-enabled Marketing Operations Leadership

**Employer need:** Practical AI adoption that improves operating leverage without separating technology from business accountability.

**Relevant proof:** public AI operating models, monitoring and intelligence systems, context architecture, source-aware workflows, QA, and governance patterns.

**Value translation:** Jared can connect AI capability to real marketing workflows, executive decisions, and repeatable operations rather than treating AI as an isolated innovation program.

## Best-fit environments

The strongest environments tend to have one or more of these conditions:

- meaningful growth investment with fragmented ownership or visibility
- multiple brands, markets, business units, or agencies
- pressure to improve acquisition economics or downstream quality
- a need to professionalize measurement, CRO, governance, or decision cadence
- an agency-to-internal capability transition
- executive demand for clearer capital-allocation and performance decisions
- an AI mandate that needs to become operating capability

## Positioning guardrail

Do not reduce the candidacy to "paid media expert." Performance media is a major source of depth, but the senior value proposition is the ability to connect performance expertise to the larger growth system and lead across the interfaces where organizations usually lose speed, clarity, and accountability.
