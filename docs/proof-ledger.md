This ledger connects the professional claim, inspectable artifact, validation surface, and business implication.

## Reading rule

The ledger is intentionally conservative. It does not turn private scale into public claims that cannot be inspected. Public artifacts prove the reusable patterns; business case studies prove the professional outcomes.
