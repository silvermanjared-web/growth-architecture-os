# Scenario: Reallocate Budget Across Channels

## Situation

Search has the strongest platform ROAS but is near demand saturation. Paid social has weaker last-click efficiency but produces higher incremental reach and improving downstream application quality. Finance asks where the next $500K should go.

## Jared's reasoning

I would reject a simple "highest ROAS wins" allocation.

The decision requires:

1. marginal rather than average efficiency;
2. available demand and saturation;
3. downstream quality;
4. incrementality;
5. payback / LTV where available;
6. scenario risk;
7. what the organization still needs to learn.

I would create allocation scenarios, protect the productive base, and reserve a defined portion of capital to resolve the uncertainty that most affects the next allocation decision.

## System behavior

- Growth Architecture OS supplies the capital-allocation and financial translation model.
- LTV/CAC Audit checks unit-economics assumptions.
- Funnel Data Validator checks downstream comparability.
- Marketing Intelligence Agent synthesizes scenarios and risks.
- Marketing Ops Toolkit can persist the reviewed recommendation and evidence.
- Full-Cycle Growth Loop turns the outcome into the next planning assumption.

## Decision shape

Not: **Search 60 / Social 40 because ROAS.**

Instead: **Protect X, reallocate Y, test Z, define the threshold that determines the next move.**

## Learning

The allocation itself becomes an experiment in marginal return, improving the next brief and forecast.
