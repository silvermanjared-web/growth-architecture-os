# ADR-001: Federation over monolith

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Keep public systems independently runnable and federate them through common language, artifacts, schemas, and optional interoperability.

## Why

A monolith would make the portfolio harder to understand, couple unrelated release cycles, and create architecture for its own sake.

## Consequence

Members can reinforce one another without requiring one another. Runtime integration must earn its complexity.
