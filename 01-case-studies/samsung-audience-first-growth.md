# Case Study: Audience-First Growth Strategy

## Context

A major consumer technology brand needed to move beyond legacy media planning and scale with stronger relevance.

## Challenge

The existing digital media approach was too rooted in keyword-first thinking. Opportunity was high, but the operating model and team routines needed to catch up.

## Approach

Rebuilt the strategy around audience-first segmentation, refined measurement models, omnichannel planning, and team enablement.

## Outcome

Managed media investment doubled to $100M within approximately six months, with the audience-first model becoming a lasting reference point. The figure is managed media investment, not client revenue or profit.

## Jared's role

Led omnichannel media strategy, stakeholder alignment, and team upskilling during a critical growth period.

## Reusable lesson

Scale requires more than budget. It requires a planning model teams can understand, activate, measure, and repeat.
