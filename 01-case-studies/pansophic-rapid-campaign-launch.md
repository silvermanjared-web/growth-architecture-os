# Case Study: Rapid Campaign Launch System

## Context

A multi-brand education environment needed fast campaign restructuring across state-level programs during a seasonal enrollment window. This work ran April–June 2025, while Jared led the Pansophic Learning account as agency lead at NP Digital. Pansophic then recruited him in-house as Senior Director, Performance Media & CRO (July 2025 – September 2026).

## Challenge

The work required speed without losing QA, pacing discipline, or cross-team visibility.

## Approach

Built a scalable launch workflow across campaign build, QA, pacing, creative refresh, and go-to-market testing.

## Outcome

Restructured 60+ campaigns in under four weeks during a high-pressure enrollment period, restoring clearer market, audience, and channel architecture while improving visibility into pacing and execution risk.

## Jared's role

As agency lead at NP Digital, led launch strategy, built QA systems, and collaborated across analytics, media, and stakeholder groups to hit aggressive timing.

## Reusable lesson

Speed only works when the operating system is clear. The faster the launch, the more important the QA and pacing model become.
